New feature: negative resources.
Some guests can have negative popularity or money. These mostly work the same as non-negative values, in that they are added to the total when a party is ended (without busting).
The player's total popularity and money can never be negative. However, anything that *would* cause the player's money to go negative, instead reduces popularity by 7 for each point it would have gone below zero. For example, if the player has 3 money, and the score of the party gives the player -7 money, then the player's money goes to 0 and he gets a -28 popularity penalty. If the player's popularity would go below 0, it instead just goes to 0 and there is no penalty.
The following new guest types have negative resources:
* Caterer: grants 4 popularity, -1 money; cost 5 popularity; named Ronald, Wendy, Mario, Tim
* Ticket Taker: grants -1 popularity, 2 money; cost 4 popularity; named Val, Grant, Mark, Stubby


New feature: entrance effects.
Some guests will cause some effect whenever they enter the party.
This feature adds one guest type with an entrance effect, but other guest types will be added later that have entirely different effects.
The new guest type is called "Climber". The climber initially grants no resources, but every time she enters the party, the popularity she grants increments by 1, to a maximum of 9. The increase in popularity only affects the specific guest that entered, not other guests of the same type. The climber costs 12 popularity. The shop has a stock of 4, named Ascella, Skye, Icarus, Vela.

New feature: overflow.
The player cannot choose to invite more guests than will fit the house's capacity, however effects may cause too many guests to be invited. If the party ever exceeds the house's capacity, then the party immediately ends without scoring, similar to when the trouble limit is exceeded, except no guest gets banned from the next party.
Two new guest types are added for this feature: Mr. Popular and Celebrity.
Mr. Popular has popularity 3, costs 5 popularity, and has an entrance effect of automatically inviting another guest.
Celebrity has popularity 2, 3 money, costs 11 popularity, and has an entrance effect of automatically inviting two more guests.
An effect that automatically invites another guest works exactly like the player inviting another guest, EXCEPT that it can overflow the party.
An entrance effect must fully complete before any other entrance effects take effect. For example, if a celebrity invites a climber as her first guest, the climber's effect doesn't happen until after the celebrity's second guest arrives. If both of the celebrity's guests have entrance effects, then they occur in the order that they arrived.
If at any point the capacity or trouble limit is exceeded, then the party ends immediately without completing any pending entrance effects.
4 of each new guest type is available in the shop.
The Mr. Populars are named Rowan, Oscar, Lawrence, and McDougall.
The Celebrities are named Troy, Rainier, Pedro, and Kent.


New feature: Star guests and winning.
The goal of the game is to have 4 stars in a party at once. Certain guests grant a star while in the party, but it does not persist past the current party, like trouble.
When a player ends a party with 4 or more stars without being shut down because of trouble or overflow, then they immediately win. Instead of proceeding to the shop phase, the player is presented with a dialog saying "Congrats! You threw the ultimate party!" The dialog has a button labeled "Victory" which ends the game and returns to the home screen.
Star guests are more expensive than other guest types. All star guests grant 1 star, although guests that grant multiple or negative stars may be added in the future.
These new guests types will be added:
- Alien: cost 40 popularity, grants 1 star, no other resources or effects. Named ET, Rocky, Olimar, and Alf.
- Leprechaun: cost 50 popularity, grants 1 star and 3 money. Named Lucky, Liam, Seamus, and Patrick.
- Dragon: costs 30 popularity, grants 1 star and -3 money. Named Smaug, Clay, Malathrax, and Toothless.
- Dinosaur: costs 25 popularity, grants 1 star and 1 trouble. Named Barney, Blue, Dino, and Rex.
- Mermaid: costs 35 popularity, grants 1 star and brings one other guest (exactly like Mr. Popular). Named Ariel, Marina, Calypso, and Oceana.
- Unicorn: costs 45 popularity, grants 1 star and 1 peace. Named Sparkles, Glitter, Stardust, and Moonbeam.
- Superhero: costs 50 popularity, grants 1 star and 3 popularity. Name Superman, Spider-man, Batman, and Iron Man.
