New feature: Guest scoring bonuses
Some guests grant bonus popularity or money depending on the content of the house. After scoring the normal popularity and money granted by the guests in the party, scoring will go through each guest and apply any bonus associated with them.
Add the following new guest types with conditional scoring bonuses:
- Comedian: Grants -1 money and costs 5 popularity. If the house is full when it is scored, then it grants 5 bonus popularity. The effect is represented by a red house emoji. Named Jerry, Nate, Bill, and Norm.
- Mascot: Grants 1 popularity and costs 5 popularity. Grants a bonus of 1 popularity for each Old Friend in the house. The effect is represented by a bold "O". Named Sparty, Gritty, Goofball, and Benny.
- Introvert: Grants 1 popularity and costs 4 popularity. Grants a bonus of 1 popularity for each empty space in the house. The effect is represented by a green house emoji. Named Sheldon, Leonard, Raj, and Howard.
- Bartender: Grants 1 popularity and costs 11 popularity. Grants a bonus of 2 money for each Trouble in the house. The effect is represented by a martini emoji. Named Sam, Woody, Diane, and Carla.
- Writer: Grants 1 popularity and costs 8 popularity. Grants a bonus of 2 popularity for each Trouble in the house. The effect is represented by a yellow X emoji. Named Franklin, Carolyn, Laura, and Erin.
